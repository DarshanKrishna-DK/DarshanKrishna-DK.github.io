# DARSHAN.WORLD design system

A cinematic personal world with useful information always within reach. Scroll moves through connected spaces, not a stack of landing-page cards. Objects should explain Darshan's work, interests or personality.

## Visual language

- Dark carbon and blue-black surfaces, warm ivory text, restrained amber UI accents.
- Pixelify Sans for game-like display type; VT323 for terminal and supporting text. Fonts are bundled locally.
- Glossy translucent controls with subtle elevation, visible focus and generous hit targets.
- Clear readable copy above the scene; never rely on decorative geometry to convey essential information.
- Circular product displays and curved passage architecture. Avoid unnecessary rectangular floating panels.

## Movement and continuity

Spawn uses a circular portal. Later passages begin with opaque arched portals; the tunnel appears after crossing. Floors and passage geometry connect throughout. World selection, copy, lighting and sound follow the same journey progress.

Gundu walks during travel and uses contextual idle actions. Accessories must fit the setting. Keep jumping clearance and prevent the companion from covering the HUD, key text or actions. Reduced-motion mode uses stable viewpoints and restrained animation.

## UI and content

Chapter controls, map, mute and resume remain easy to find. Dialogs must have a clear close action, managed focus and room to scroll. The Campfire has one email action, followed closely by socials; do not duplicate the address as display text.

At compact viewport heights prioritize readable content and reachable controls over decorative scale. Inspect desktop and phone compositions after scene or typography changes. Lightweight mode must remain an intentional design, with Gundu still in 3D when graphics are available.

## Sound

A shared score connects Spawn through DevRel. Applause occurs once after a three-second auditorium stay. Road uses breeze and birds; Campfire uses fire, wind and nighttime nature. Audio requires user interaction, crossfades between settings and has an obvious mute control.
